# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Small UAE B2B and service businesses with 3 to 15 salespeople. The first client showcase is a Dubai aesthetic and dental clinic. Two people matter:

- **The owner** buys the product. They open it to see whether enquiries become revenue and whether the team follows up.
- **The rep** must like it, or adoption dies. Reps (patient coordinators, in the showcase) live on WhatsApp and their phones. Their job on any screen is to answer the next enquiry quickly, move a deal forward, and never forget a follow-up.

## Product Purpose

HCO CRM is a sales inbox with a deal pipeline. Leads arrive by themselves from Facebook and Instagram lead ads, TikTok forms and WhatsApp. Reps reply from inside the CRM on the channel the lead came from, and every message and action lands on a timeline. Deals move through a pipeline, and the system makes sure nobody drops a follow-up.

Success is an owner who can see which channels bring real business, and reps who choose to work in it all day instead of their personal WhatsApp.

## Positioning

The rep never re-enters data they already typed somewhere else: WhatsApp-first selling for UAE teams, with AED, VAT and TRN built in.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, local. Quiet confidence rather than hype. It should feel made for businesses in the UAE (AED amounts, +971 numbers, Arabic and English names side by side, emirates and free zones) without leaning on clichés.

References, and what to take from each:

- **Linear:** speed, restraint and crisp typography.
- **Stripe Dashboard:** clarity and trust around money and numbers.
- **Attio:** calm, modern record pages and lists that stay readable while dense.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS template: purple gradients, identical card grids, stock hero metrics.
- A WhatsApp clone: a green chat-app skin that hides that this is a sales tool.
- A dark hacker or terminal look.

## Design Principles

1. **Never make the rep type twice.** Prefill from what the lead, message or form already said; every screen should shorten the path to the next reply or the next stage.
2. **The next action is obvious.** Each screen leads with what needs doing now (reply, call, move, send), and the rest recedes.
3. **Money and time are exact.** AED amounts, VAT, TRN and Dubai-time timestamps are shown precisely and consistently, because owners trust numbers first.
4. **Channel is context, not decoration.** Where a lead came from and which conversation window is open are always visible, stated plainly.
5. **Demo-ready at every step.** Anything a client can touch must look finished, on a laptop and on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target has been set yet. Sensible defaults apply: readable contrast, labelled controls, keyboard-reachable actions and respect for reduced motion. Revisit when the pilot starts.
